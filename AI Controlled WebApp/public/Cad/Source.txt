SO101Parts.glb is a derived display asset. Original STL visual origins are baked
into link-local meshes; original joint transforms are preserved separately in
the application's CadModel.json. The application applies its own materials.

SO101.urdf is a source reference. Its source STL dependencies are available in
the original repository and the Downloads/SO101Cad package; this GUI asset
directory is not a standalone URDF distribution.
